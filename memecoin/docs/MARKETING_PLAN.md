# Marketing: the part that actually determines "success"

Security hygiene (`LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`) prevents the avoidable failure
modes. It doesn't create demand. For a memecoin specifically, demand is
close to the entire game - there's usually no product, so the token's
value is a direct function of how many people know it exists, trust it,
and want in. That's marketing and community work, not code, and no
script can do it for you. This is a checklist of legitimate tactics, not
an automation tool.

## Before launch

- [ ] A one-line premise anyone can repeat in one sentence. If you can't
      explain why this coin exists and not another one in one sentence,
      neither can anyone sharing it.
- [ ] Landing page live (see `site/index.html`) with the real mint
      address slotted in the moment it's minted
- [ ] X/Twitter and Telegram (or Discord) set up *before* launch, not
      scrambled together after - people checking a brand-new token expect
      to find both
- [ ] A few days of genuine content (the premise, the art/meme, why
      you're building it) posted before the token exists, so the account
      isn't founded the same hour as the launch (an extremely common rug
      pattern that experienced traders explicitly screen for)

## Launch

- [ ] Post the mint address alongside the verifiable facts from
      `LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` (authorities revoked, LP locked/burned,
      supply allocation) - lead with the proof, not just hype
- [ ] Pin the launch post
- [ ] Personally reply to early questions - the first hour of real
      engagement matters more than volume of posts

## Ongoing (the part that's actually hard)

- [ ] Consistent posting cadence - memecoin communities are attention-
      driven and go quiet fast without regular content
- [ ] Engage with people talking about the coin rather than only
      broadcasting - reply, don't just post
- [ ] Be honest about setbacks. A community that catches you spinning
      bad news stops trusting the good news too
- [ ] Track holder count and volume over time (Solscan/DEX aggregators
      expose both for free) so you know if what you're doing is working,
      not just how it feels

## Do not do (illegal and/or destroys trust the moment it's discovered)

- Buying followers, engagement, or fake volume
- Coordinating pump-and-dump timing with a private group
- Claiming partnerships, audits, or team credentials that don't exist
- Multiple anonymous accounts posing as unrelated people hyping the same
  coin ("astroturfing")

None of this is scriptable in a way that's honest, and the moment any of
it is discovered publicly (and it usually is - on-chain activity and
account histories are both public), it does more damage than not
marketing at all.
