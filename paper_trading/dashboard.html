Robustness (walk-forward)

—

The full-backtest numbers above are one long in-sample fit - a strategy can look great by luck alone over one history. Walk-forward re-optimizes each strategy's parameters on rolling training windows and scores it only on data it never saw during that fit. A strategy that's robust here made money on genuinely unseen data, not just in hindsight. Param stability is a separate check: the % of nearby parameter values (e.g. a lookback of 18 or 22 instead of 20) that were also profitable - a real edge tends to hold up across a plateau of similar settings, not just the one exact default.

🚩 Rug Pull Watch

—

Flags coins with a severe 24h decline or drawdown from their own 24h high - the price-action aftermath a rug pull leaves behind (liquidity drained, price collapses), not the on-chain cause. This exchange is a curated, mainstream listing where a literal on-chain rug pull (LP drain, honeypot contract) is rare - most flags here will be ordinary high-volatility drawdowns, a broad market selloff, or a thin-liquidity air pocket, not an actual rug. There's no holder-concentration or liquidity-lock data behind this - it's a crash proxy from price/volume alone, not a verdict.
