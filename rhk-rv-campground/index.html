About the Park

A quiet, secure place to settle in.

RHK RV Campground Park opened in the fall of 2019 under RHK Holdings Ltd., built as a mid-to-long-term, all-season RV community in Parkland County, Alberta. It's set up for people who want to park their rig for a season or longer, not just pass through for a night — fenced, gated, and watched over by security cameras, with each lot serviced so you're not hunting for a fresh water fill or a dump station.
