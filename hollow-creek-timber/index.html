Our Story

Three generations, one woodlot.

Earl Whitfield opened the mill in 1987 with a single circular saw and forty acres of hardwood behind his house. His daughter Dana took over in 2009 and built the first kiln; her son Micah now runs the yard day-to-day, and still hand-picks every live-edge slab himself.

We select-cut our own woodlot on a rotation, and for every tree we take, we plant one back. Most of what's in the yard today was standing timber ten years ago.
